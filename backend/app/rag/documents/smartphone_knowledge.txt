SMARTPHONE PRODUCT KNOWLEDGE

DISPLAY

AMOLED and OLED displays generally provide deeper blacks, higher contrast,
and more vibrant colors than conventional LCD panels.

Higher refresh rates such as 90Hz, 120Hz, and 144Hz generally provide
smoother scrolling and animations than 60Hz displays.

A high refresh rate does not automatically mean that the display has
better image quality. Panel technology, brightness, resolution, and color
accuracy also matter.

BATTERY

Larger battery capacity generally provides greater potential battery
runtime, but actual battery life depends on processor efficiency, display
refresh rate, brightness, software optimization, and usage.

A 5000mAh battery is generally considered a substantial smartphone battery.
Batteries around 6000mAh or higher can provide particularly strong
battery capacity when combined with efficient hardware.

CHARGING

Higher charging wattage can reduce charging time, although actual charging
speed depends on the charging protocol, battery management, temperature,
charger, and device implementation.

CAMERA

Camera megapixel count alone does not determine camera quality.

Image processing, sensor size, optical stabilization, lens quality,
software algorithms, and lighting conditions also influence photography.

PROCESSOR

A smartphone processor affects application performance, gaming capability,
power efficiency, image processing, and overall responsiveness.

Processor performance should be evaluated using the processor generation,
architecture, CPU/GPU capabilities, and the target usage rather than only
the processor name.

RAM

Higher RAM capacity can help with multitasking and keeping applications
available in memory.

For typical smartphone usage, 8GB RAM provides a strong multitasking
baseline, while higher capacities can be useful for demanding workloads.

STORAGE

Higher storage capacity allows users to store more applications, photos,
videos, and files.

256GB provides substantially more storage capacity than 128GB and can be
useful for users who store large amounts of media.

5G

5G connectivity can provide higher network speeds and lower latency where
compatible network coverage is available.

The real-world benefit depends on the carrier, coverage, network
conditions, and supported bands.

WATER AND DUST RESISTANCE

IP ratings describe protection against dust and water under specified
testing conditions.

A higher IP rating generally indicates stronger tested resistance, but
water resistance does not mean a device is completely waterproof.

WEIGHT

Larger batteries, larger displays, and additional hardware can increase
smartphone weight.

A heavier device may provide advantages such as larger battery capacity
but can be less comfortable for prolonged one-handed use.
